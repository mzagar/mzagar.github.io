About

I'm a software engineer at Infobip. Most of my work is backend: distributed systems, messaging infrastructure, and the developer tooling around them.

Lately I've been spending time on agents and MCP servers — small, well-defined tools that a model can actually use. It is a good excuse to care about interfaces again.

I live in Donja Stubica, a small town in the Zagorje hills about 30 km north of Zagreb.

Outside of work: triathlon and deep-sky photography. Both are slower than software. You train for months for a few minutes of racing, and you wait all night for a few hours of clear sky. I like that.

The best way to reach me is on GitHub or LinkedIn.
